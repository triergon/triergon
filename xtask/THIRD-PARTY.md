`xtask` is repository tooling. It is never linked into a library crate; no
package in the workspace may depend on it (`cargo xtask check-safety` enforces
this). Two of its modules derive from other software and keep their notices.

## zlib (`xtask/src/zlib.rs`)

`trees.c`), used only to regenerate the authored zlib test fixtures byte for
byte. It is not the original software.

## Mersenne Twister and CPython `random` (`xtask/src/pyrandom.rs`)

MT19937 with its reference initialisation, seeded and drawn from as CPython's
`random` module does, used to reproduce the seeded byte stream of an authored
fixture and the bootstrap resampling and pair order of recorded measurements.
Changes from CPython: a Rust rendering of only these operations.
